# Prior work

## Artifact 1 — output-control audit

[*Models Answer a Different Question*](https://github.com/alexanderhayden/identification-under-context) audited an output-control task and its scoring assumptions. It found ordinary response strategies that could mimic or produce apparent output-control failures, including categorical selection of the requested majority and stable lexical attractors. Direct follow-up audits examined provider routing, parsing, scaffold effects, ACT-vs-SIMULATE framing, context placement, and training-stage comparisons.

Artifact 1 contains benchmark-derived materials under its own protection rules. This repository does not copy its prompts, protected word lists, encrypted archives, raw data, or experiment-specific analyses.

## Relationship to this repository

Directive Tests treats the earlier findings as motivation and known prior information, not as new evidence. Reusable engineering concepts were cleanly refactored: provider identity capture, local base/chat separation, retry accounting, raw JSONL retention, immutable scheduling, and manifest provenance.
